Program Overview

About the Summer School

The DIGIT Africa Summer School 2026 brings together two intensive hands-on workshops designed for researchers and practitioners in networking and data science. Participants gain practical experience and knowledge through theoretical background and state-of-the-art instrumentation and tools.

What makes a 5G experiment reproducible? How do recommendation algorithms drive polarisation in social networks?
🎯

Two Parallel Workshops

A full-day program covering 5G network deployment with SLICES-RI and epistemic enclave detection with SoBigData tools.

🛠️

Key Tools & Platforms

CDlib, NDlib, YSocial for network analysis — OpenAirInterface, Docker, and SLICES-RI for 5G experimentation.

⏱️

12 Hours of Training

Two 6-hour sessions combining theory and hands-on practice, with coffee breaks and a lunch break included.

🎓

For PhD Students

Intermediate Python and Linux knowledge recommended. Preconfigured environments provided on both platforms — no complex setup required.

Workshop Details

Workshop Descriptions

Two parallel tracks, each 6 hours, combining theory and extensive hands-on practice on real infrastructures.

A

SLICES-RI Post-5G Blueprint Hands-on

Hands-on tutorial covering 5G architecture, Docker-based deployment, and measurement workflows on the SLICES-RI infrastructure.

5G Docker SLICES-RI OpenAirInterface

Modules
🌅 Morning — Background on 5G & Reproducibility
Module 1 · 9:30 – 11:00
Foundations — 5G principles and architecture. Understanding the interactions of core functions and RAN components composing a 5G network.
Module 2 · 11:15 – 12:45
Reproducible experimental research with SLICES-RI. Setup a research environment and run a first hello-world experiment with data collection and publication.
🌆 Afternoon — 5G Experiments
Module 3 · 13:45 – 15:15
Deploy your own 5G network with SLICES-RI. Configure 5G core and RAN, then analyze signaling traffic and compare it to theory.
Module 4 · 15:30 – 17:00
Traffic workloads on 5G and analysis. Connect UEs to the network, run experiments, and design your own analysis scenario.

B

Hands-on Training on the SoBigData Platform

Identifying Epistemic Enclaves and Understanding Polarisation. By Giulio Rossetti (CNR-ISTI) and Andrea Failla (Italian National PhD Program in AI).

CDlib NDlib YSocial NetworkX
6 Hours
Duration
Python
Requirement
4
Modules
🌅 Morning — The Anatomy of Segregation
Module 1 · 9:30 – 11:00
Foundations with NetworkX. Load, manipulate and explore complex networks. Metric analysis: degree distribution, clustering coefficients, path lengths.
Module 2 · 11:15 – 12:45
Detecting Epistemic Enclaves with CDlib. Apply Louvain, Leiden, Infomap, Label Propagation. Evaluate community cohesion, isolation, and fragmentation.
🌆 Afternoon — The Mechanics of Division
Module 3 · 13:45 – 15:15
Simulating Polarisation with NDlib. Explore Deffuant–Weisbuch and Algorithmic Bias models. Observe phase transitions: consensus, disagreement, polarisation.
Module 4 · 15:30 – 17:00
The Controlled Sandbox with YSocial. Generate synthetic networks and characterize simulation data using CDlib and NDlib to test what-if scenarios.

Technical Prerequisites
Participants should have the following background to get the most out of the workshops.
Intermediate Python (3.10+): comfortable with functions, loops, data structures.
Linux basics (for Workshop A): SSH, command-line navigation, file permissions.
Jupyter Notebook experience: opening, running cells, understanding markdown.
Curiosity about 5G or social networks: no specific domain knowledge required!
Software Setup
Preconfigured JupyterLab environments available on SoBigData RI and SLICES-RI. Participants need only an SSH client and browser.

// Research Overview

About the Task

The Summer school 2026 Shared Task addresses stance detection in polarized social media discourse on the Palestinian-Israeli conflict and related regional issues, introducing a novel dual-framework evaluation.

Participants will develop models to analyze social media posts across two subtasks: Subtask A (Actor-Level Stance Detection) identifies whether authors lean Pro-Palestine, Pro-Israel, or remain Neutral, while Subtask B (Cross-Topic Stance Detection) classifies positions on specific regional issues (Normalization with Israel, Refugee/Immigrant Presence) as Favor, Against, or Neither.

This dual-framework enables investigation of fundamental questions: How do general political alignments (actors) relate to positions on specific issues (targets)? Can a unified model handle diverse, context-dependent stances?
🎯

Dual Framework

Distinguishes between actor-level political alignments and cross-topic stance patterns across two conflict-related subjects.

🌐

Bilingual Dataset

English data for Subtask A, Arabic data for Subtask B — enabling multilingual NLP research in politically sensitive domains.

📊

2,606 Samples

Carefully curated and annotated social media posts spanning multiple topics, stances, and linguistic registers.

🤝

Open Participation

Free to join. Participants from academia and industry are welcome, individually or as a team. No prior registration fee required.

// Performance Metrics

Evaluation Metrics

Systems will be evaluated using standard classification metrics, with Macro F1 as the primary ranking criterion.

Primary Metric
Macro F1
Teams ranked primarily by this score — ideal for multi-class problems with balanced performance emphasis.
Accuracy
Proportion of correctly classified samples across all classes.
Precision
Ratio of true positives to predicted positives, per class.
Recall
Ratio of true positives to actual positives, per class.
Ranking: Teams will be ranked primarily by Macro F1 score. This metric is particularly suitable for multi-class problems and emphasizes balanced performance across all classes.
